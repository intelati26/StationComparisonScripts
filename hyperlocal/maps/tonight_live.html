925 mb wind at the site by hour (mph). The equation's wind term halves near 25 mph and is nearly gone by 35 mph.
Set a place and press go for the low.

The equation: drop = amplitude x night x exp(-(U925/30 mph)^3) x (1 - 0.8 x cloud) x min(1, (T - Td)/12 F), with T, Td, cloud and wind from the HRRR (GFS outside the US) and the dewpoint corrected 1 F (Jun-Aug), 3 F (Sep-Nov) or 2 F. Amplitude 5 F is the typical site of 29 compared; 10 F is a cold-prone hollow. See EQUATION.md.
